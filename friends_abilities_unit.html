🔊 语速：
A · How are your friends different?
Let's talk + Let's learn
Hi, class. You'll have a new classmate. He's from Australia. His name is Oliver.
同学们好。你们将有一位新同学。他来自澳大利亚，他叫 Oliver。
What's he like?
他是什么样的人？
He's tall and strong.
他又高又强壮。
Is he good at football?
他擅长踢足球吗？
Yes, I think he is. He can run very fast.
是的，我觉得他很擅长。他跑得很快。
Oliver can't speak Chinese. Would you like to help him, Zhang Peng?
Oliver 不会说中文。张鹏，你愿意帮助他吗？

B · What can you do?
Let's talk + Let's learn
The Science and Art Festival is in two weeks.
科技艺术节两周后举行。
What can you do for the festival?
你能为艺术节做什么？
I can make a robot.
我会制作机器人。
Can you make robots, Sarah?
Sarah，你会制作机器人吗？
No, I can't.
不，我不会。
I can dance.
我会跳舞。
能力词组 · 点一下听
核心句型：
What can you do? → I can ...
Can you ...? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Let's spell · ai / ay
Listen, repeat and chant
ai    ay
The dog likes to play in the rain.
这只狗喜欢在雨中玩耍。
He waits for the boy to come and play.
它等着男孩过来一起玩。
The happy little dog wags his tail.
这只快乐的小狗摇着它的尾巴。
They play games on a happy wet day.
他们在一个快乐的雨天一起玩游戏。
发音观察：教材这一页把 ai 与 ay 放在一起练习。多听 rain / wait / tail 和 play / day / say 的发音。

Reading time · Grace
阅读理解
We have a new student in our class today. Her name's Grace.
今天我们班来了一位新同学。她叫 Grace。
Hi, everyone. Nice to meet you all!
大家好！很高兴认识大家！
Grace is so lovely and friendly.
Grace 很可爱，也很友好。
Yes, I can draw really well.
是的，我画画画得很好。
Thank you for your help, Grace.
谢谢你的帮助，Grace。
You're welcome.
不客气。
阅读要点：Grace 虽然不能和同学们一起踢足球，但她能画画，并为足球比赛画了海报。教材用这个故事继续练习 “人物特点 + 能力”。
